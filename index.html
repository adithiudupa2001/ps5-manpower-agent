Daily Manpower Report

MGMT6110 PS5 · Adithi · Group 4

The Business Problem

Every morning, someone in HR compiles the manpower report by hand: pulling punch data, matching it against approved leave, and working out who is present, on leave or absent. That report has to reach the time office, the manpower head and leadership before the day starts. It is repetitive, error-prone and late on busy days. My agent puts that daily manpower view straight into their inbox, every morning, with no manual work.

Output: a daily manpower report in the inbox: headcount, present, on leave, unplanned absence and absenteeism %, flagged when it crosses the limit.

Collaboration Log & Reflection

AI delegation, verification methods, errors caught, and evaluation

Delegated to AI

Claude helped choose the use case, design the sheet formulas, generate fictional data and draft content; Google AI Studio built this page.

Checked

Every email against the sheet's Answer Key and Zap history.

Errors Caught

The AI gave the wrong day of the week; it told me to delete test rows, then corrected itself; my schedule showed UTC because Singapore time was set on a different Zapier account.

Time and Cost

6 hours, $0 (Zapier free plan).

Reflection

Counting attendance fitted the agent because the answer is checkable. It failed where a number needed context: 12% looked normal until I added a status flag. With a paid plan I'd pull live punch data and add a team breakdown.
